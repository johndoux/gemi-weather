# Privacy Policy for Gemi Weather

*Last updated: May 31, 2026*

## Overview

Gemi Weather is a simple weather app for families. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information.

## Location Data

Gemi Weather may request access to your device's location to show you local weather. Here is how that data is handled:

- Your location is used only to fetch the current weather for your area.
- Your last known location (coordinates and city name) is saved in encrypted storage on your device so the app continues to work if location permission is later turned off.
- Your location is never transmitted to the developer or any third party beyond the weather service described below.
- Location permission is optional. You can type a city or zip code instead.

## Weather Data

Weather is fetched from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), a free and open-source weather API. Only your coordinates are sent — no name, email, device ID, or any other identifying information. Open-Meteo's privacy policy is available at their website.

## Analytics & Tracking

Gemi Weather does not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking tools. No data about your usage of the app is collected or sent anywhere.

## Children's Privacy

Gemi Weather is designed for use with young children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else.
